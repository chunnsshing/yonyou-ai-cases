How this was counted. Built from the local copy of retail_vehicle, read through flat_retail_vehicle — the flattened index of it — which holds 47,743 rows covering 2025-01-02 to 2026-10-06. This page shows 2025-01-02 to 2026-10-06. One row is one line item, so visits are distinct document numbers and never rows. Today means 2026-10-06, the last day the copy holds, so "days ago" does not drift with the wall clock. The average visit interval uses de-duplicated calendar days (last − first) ÷ (distinct days − 1): a fleet account raises dozens of documents in one day, and counting documents instead collapses the interval to nearly zero and turns every warning red. Segments are computed over everything held, not the window, so a label does not move when the range changes. Walk-ins (CASH RETAIL, CASH FLEET, CASH RETAIL (WORKSHOP)) are counted in revenue but never ranked as customers; a walk-in is carried by its vehicle. Makes are typed by hand and only unambiguous spellings were merged. Nothing was fetched from YonSuite and nothing was written to it.
