How to read this report

Gap

Gap = (price paid − market price) ÷ market price, measured against the market on the PO date, so it judges the purchase rather than later market moves. "vs today" shows what the same order would cost now.

Status bands

Tolerance depends on how close the benchmark is to what was bought (Exact spec ±5%, Grade proxy ±10%, Broad proxy ±20%). Above market > 2× tolerance; Watch > 1×; In line within; Below market below −1×; Far below – verify below −2×, which usually means a unit, currency, tax or spec mismatch. A broad proxy, or today's price against a PO older than 30 days, can never score above Watch.

Money figures

Above benchmark now: value of the latest PO line above what the same quantity cost at benchmark, only where the gap is outside tolerance. Target price: today's benchmark + tolerance. Saving at target: (price paid − target) × run-rate annual quantity. Run-rate: window spend × 365 ÷ window days; it assumes buying continues at the same pace. Cost at risk: run-rate × 10%. These are estimates for prioritising, not claims of supplier overcharging: benchmarks are public reference prices and exclude freight, premiums and payment terms.

Insights and actions

Per-material insights are calculated from the data: position vs benchmark, market move since the PO, supplier price changes vs the benchmark over the same dates (pass-through, ±3 pts treated as noise), where today's benchmark sits in its recent range, price spread between suppliers, and the direction of recent news. Outlook is the direction published by the cited source, or the benchmark's 30-day move (±3% = flat). Actions follow a fixed rule from price position × outlook.

Sources

Market prices come from international exchanges and price publishers; news from international outlets (see DEFAULT_SOURCES in the script). China-domestic sites are blocked by default. Every price, outlook and article links to its source.
